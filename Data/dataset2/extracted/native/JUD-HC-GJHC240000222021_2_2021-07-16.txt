ORAL ORDER
 (PER : HONOURABLE MR. JUSTICE A.J.DESAI)
1. Ms. Vrunda Shah, learned AGP, states that the judgement and
order dated 10.01.2020 passed by the learned Single Judge
and Special Civil Application No. 17182 of 2016 is under
challenge by way of filing appeal under Clause – 15 of the
Letters Patent Appeal. The Appeal was lastly listed on
07.04.2021. An application for early hearing of the appeal
was moved requesting for stay of operation of the judgement
and order passed by the learned Single Judge. Request for
time is made by the learned AGP.
2. Mr. Kunal Vyas, learned advocate appearing for Nanavati
Associates appearing for the applicant, has opposed such
request.

3. Considering the submission made by the learned advocates
appearing for the respective parties and the fact that the
appeal is pending for hearing, the following order is passed. 
4. The respondent – State Authority is hereby directed to deposit
the amount before the Registry within a period of four weeks.
The amount shall be paid by an A/c Payee cheque to the
applicant after a period of eight weeks herein after in absence
of any stay order granted by the Division Bench of this Court
in Letters Patent Appeal.
5. It would be open for the applicant to file a fresh application,
in case of difficulty.
6. Non-compliance of the order passed by the learned Single
Judge shall be viewed very seriously.
7. With the above direction, present application stands disposed
of.
